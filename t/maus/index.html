OpenMausBot

Run your own team of AI bots from a free, open source chat app.

Open OpenMausBot by Milind Soni and OpenMausBot contributors

What it is

OpenMausBot (also called MausBot) is an open source alternative to Grok Bot. It gives you a team of AI bots in a chat app on your own computer. It is made by Milind Soni and contributors, and it is licensed under Apache 2.0.

What it can do

  • Give each bot a job, a model and its own computer
  • Switch a bot's model mid-conversation
  • Ask for your approval before bots act
  • Connect apps like Gmail, Slack, GitHub and Notion
  • Run work on a schedule or by webhook
  • Run bots on your own machine with local data

What you could use it for

Daily planning helper

You could create a bot that helps plan your day or drafts an email. You review the result before anything is sent.

Support inbox triage

You could connect Gmail and set up a bot to sort incoming messages. It asks for your approval before it acts.

Bug fixing helper

You could connect GitHub and give a bot the job of working on bugs. You allow or deny each step.

Scheduled competitor watch

You could set a bot to run on a schedule and keep an eye on competitors. You choose which model it uses.

How to start

  1. Download the desktop app for your computer from the official site
  2. Install and open the app
  3. Connect one provider, such as Claude Code or Codex
  4. Create your first bot: pick a name, engine and model
  5. Send a small task and review the result

What it costs

Good to know: The app is free and open source, but you need your own AI account or API key, and that provider's fees and limits apply. Optional cloud plans start at $15/month.
